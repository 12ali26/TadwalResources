One route plan for the whole corridor

The South Coast is a long, linear market — a single road spine feeding beach resorts, trading centres and rural retail. We run that spine end to end so no outlet is too far from a delivery day.

  • Scheduled routes covering the full Likoni–Lungalunga corridor
  • Supply to resorts, bars and restaurants in Diani & Ukunda
  • Wholesale and duka supply inland to Kwale and Kinango
  • Chilled and ambient stock, returns and empties handled
