The product

Built for the coast, not adapted to it

Salt air, humidity and hard sun break down ordinary paint fast. Ocean 53 is formulated for exactly these conditions, which is why it holds colour and film integrity on coastal buildings where other coatings chalk and peel.

  • Resilience against coastal humidity and salt exposure
  • UV and extreme-heat stability for long recoat intervals
  • Interior, exterior and protective lines for one supplier
