About us

A supply-chain bridge for coastal & southern Kenya

Tadwal Resources is a robust, multi-category distribution and logistics company operating across the coastal and southern interior regions of Kenya. We connect major manufacturers with the commercial buyers, retailers, hardware stores, institutions and end-consumers who rely on their products.

What we do

One operation, four product categories

Most distributors specialise in a single category. We run four — LPG, dairy, beverages and industrial coatings — because the hard asset underneath them all is the same: an owned fleet, a depot network and disciplined route planning across a wide region.

That scale lets a small hardware store in Voi, a supermarket in Nyali, a beach resort in Diani and a school kitchen in Loitoktok all deal with one dependable partner.

  • LPG — Taifa Gas across Taita Taveta & Kajiado
  • Dairy — Daima Milk across Mombasa
  • Beverages — exclusive Pepsi mandate, South Coast
  • Coatings — exclusive Ocean 53 distributor, plus contracting
