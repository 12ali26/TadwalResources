The fleet

Two vehicle classes, one dispatch

Different products and different legs of the journey need different vehicles. We run both ends of that range in-house.

Mitsubishi Fuso heavy trucks

For bulk, long-haul regional transit — moving full loads from coastal supply points into the interior towns of Taita Taveta, Kajiado and the Makueni corridor.

  • Bulk LPG and palletised FMCG loads
  • Long-distance regional corridors
  • Depot-to-depot replenishment runs

Pickups & Proboxes

For rapid last-mile delivery — threading into markets, estates, trading centres and roadside retail where the big trucks can't efficiently go.

  • Daily retail routes and reorders
  • Market and estate access
  • Fast turnaround on urgent top-ups
