What we do

Two depots, positioned where the volume is

Fresh dairy lives and dies by turnaround. Our Kongowea depot sits beside one of the busiest markets on the coast; our Likoni depot serves the south side and the ferry corridor. Between them we cover adjacent zones on short, frequent routes.

  • Cold-chain storage and handling at both depots
  • Daily last-mile routes to shops, kiosks and supermarkets
  • Full Daima range — long-life, fresh and cultured lines
  • Stock rotation and returns handled with the retailer
